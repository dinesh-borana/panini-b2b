## What we're building

A public, shareable B2B catalog platform for Panini Jewels that complements (not replaces) the WhatsApp-based sales channel. Buyers can browse Panini's design library, share products to their own WhatsApp contacts, send selections back to Panini's team, or place orders directly on the platform.

## What Panini Jewels sells

Fashion/imitation/artificial jewelry wholesale. Per-design flat pricing. Materials: alloys, brass, plated metals, German silver, kundan-style stones, AD/CZ stones, etc.

## Why now

~1000 buyers across 4 WhatsApp groups, growing. Two scaling walls:
1. Volume of repetitive "rate kya hai" questions
2. No standardized catalog for onboarding new retailers

## Strategy: Hybrid (catalog-first)

WhatsApp stays as the transaction layer. Platform is the discovery layer. Team replaces "type the price" with "send the link."

## Core principles
1. No login to browse
2. WhatsApp-first sharing
3. Show only what's available (out-of-stock hidden)
4. Mobile-first
5. Catalog completeness over checkout polish

## Success metrics (60d post-launch, tiered)

| Metric | Floor | Target | Stretch |
|---|---|---|---|
| % WhatsApp buyers visit catalog ≥2x | 15% | 30% | 50% |
| % enquiries reference catalog item | 10% | 20% | 35% |
| Median onboarding time | <3d | <24h | Same-day |

## Biggest risk: Buyer adoption

Mitigations: every team reply includes link, catalog must be better than scrolling WhatsApp, one-tap reshare to WhatsApp, designs not in WhatsApp groups (incentive).

## Constraints
- Founder + 1 team member
- No timeline/budget pressure
- MyBillBook for billing (no MVP integration)
- Single-tenant for Panini

## Out of scope for v1
- Multi-tenancy, MyBillBook integration, full e-commerce (cart/wishlist/history) → Phase 2
- Pending order management → Phase 2 (data model accommodates)
- Image search, order book scanning → Phase 2
- B2C, mobile native, real-time chat, multi-language, gold rate display
